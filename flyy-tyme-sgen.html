SGEN 07 / 11

What we want to learn from you

The questions we'd love your read on.

  1. Where's the website build today — who's doing it, how far along, what's working, what isn't? Tells us whether we're augmenting, displacing, or starting clean.
  2. One site or two? FLYY TYME and SADDBERRYS under one roof, or separate properties? Drives architecture and changes everything downstream.
  3. Where are you on cultivation and licensing in Nevada? Determines what compliance and dispensary work makes sense to scope in.
  4. What does Phase 1 actually look like — first sale, first event, first investor close? Sets the urgency and the success markers.
  5. What's the biggest thing blocking you right now? Sometimes the answer is design, sometimes traffic, sometimes capital, sometimes a person.
  6. If we partnered, what would month 6 feel like for it to have been worth doing? Aligns us on what we're actually building toward.
