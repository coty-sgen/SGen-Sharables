🔁 Better options for your setup & goals

You're building a one-bag creator + remote-work kit for a hot, humid climate. A few swaps that save money, weight, or headaches:

  • OP-1 cover → Decksaver polycarbonate (not acrylic). Same price ballpark, won't crack in a backpack. Already linked above.
  • Drop one travel adapter. Keep the Epicka Pulse Duo 65W (it can charge a laptop + phone); skip the 40W. Saves ~$27 + space.
  • AirTag 2 → original AirTag 4-pack if these are just for luggage/bags. The originals routinely hit ~$45–60 vs ~$99, and tracking accuracy for a suitcase is identical in practice. Saves ~$40.
  • Rethink the dual-camera harness. With a GoPro + phone gimbal + drone, a two-camera leather harness is a lot of bulk for a setup that isn't really "two big cameras." Skip unless you're running a mirrorless body too. Saves ~$35 + weight.
  • Charging consolidation. The Anker MagGo bank already props your phone up and charges it. A separate 3-in-1 station (KU XIU) is nice at a fixed home base but redundant if you're truly mobile. Pick one based on whether you'll have a stable desk in Bali.
  • UPERFECT 4K monitor is heavy + costly (~1.8 lb + case). For sales/BD work you rarely need 4K. A 1080p OLED 15.6" portable (e.g. ViewSonic VG1656 / ASUS ZenScreen / KYY) is lighter and often $120–180 — frees ~$150 and grams. Keep the 4K only if you do photo/video color work.
  • Aer Travel Pack 4 → Travel Pack 4 Ultra (X-Pac) is meaningfully lighter for the same 35L — worth it for one-bag + the 7kg airline limit below. Costs a bit more, but weight is the currency that matters when flying budget carriers in SE Asia.
  • Roost V3 is excellent and you won't find a real discount — but if it's just for a laptop at cafés, a $15–25 folding aluminum stand (Nexstand K2 / MOFT) does 90% of the job for a fraction. Keep the Roost only if you value the rigidity.

🌏 Two Bali / SE-Asia flags worth more than any coupon

1. Your drone needs to be registered in Indonesia. Indonesia requires drone registration and restricts where you can fly (Bali has no-fly zones around temples, airports and beaches). Bringing the HoverAir X1 Pro in unregistered risks confiscation. Verify current rules with an Indonesia source before you fly — flagged as to-verify, not confirmed here.
2. Budget airlines cap carry-on at ~7 kg. AirAsia, Lion Air, Scoot etc. weigh cabin bags. A loaded 35L Aer pack can hit 9–11 kg with all this gear — meaning paid checked bags or gate repacking on every hop. This is the strongest argument for the lighter Travel Pack 4 Ultra and for trimming the kit above.

Voltage is fine: Indonesia is 230V / Type C & F plugs, and every device here is USB / dual-voltage. Your adapters cover it.
